SPIKE NOTES
  • Textures sliced from the contact sheet into uploads/texture/. The source had no alpha channel - transparency was a painted checkerboard - so it was keyed out, flood-filled from the borders, and edge-decontaminated to stop white fringing on the tears.
  • Palette is sampled from the assets, not eyeballed, so the CSS paper matches the photographed paper.
  • The paper body and the dark band are mirror-tiled patches cut from the assets' interiors - the raw crops drag their own torn borders into view when scaled to cover.
  • Total texture weight: ~444 KB in WebP across five files.
