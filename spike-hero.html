The Commission Process

The Brief

You tell me the scope, the tone, and the deadline. I come back with questions worth asking before anyone writes a word.

SPIKE NOTES - RHYTHM
  • Boundaries alternate: torn (hero→stats, supplements→process, business→workshop) and clean (stats→supplements, process→business, workshop→footer). Three of each, so the tear stays punctuation.
  • All three tears come from one 6 KB mask, varied by --tear-h (40/56/72px), --tear-w (100/132/168%), --tear-x offset, and one mirrored copy.
  • Grounds alternate map / dark / paper, with the map returning once at the workshop as a deliberate callback. Body copy never sits directly on the map.
  • Adding a torn boundary is two classes: .torn on the section above, .tuck on the one below with a matching offset.
